What was actually broken was normalised power, on both rides

Normalised power is meant to be the steady wattage metabolically equivalent to a variable ride, which gives it a hard ceiling: it cannot exceed the hardest 20 minutes you actually averaged, because that 20-minute average is a steady effort you really produced. Yesterday it read 271 W against a best 20-minute average of 217 W. On Sep 7 it read 243 W against 220 W. Both impossible, and both for the same reason — normalised power is inflated by its own 30-second rolling and fourth-power weighting on stop-start, spiky, heavy-coasting rides. Yesterday had 404 m of climbing in 24.6 km, 13% of the time stopped and a fifth of it out of the saddle. So intensity factor and training-stress score are now left blank on both rides by rule, with a reason attached, and average power plus the full power curve are kept and used instead.
